# Literature sweep for the ARC-AGI-3 write-up (composer's subagent, 2026-10-25; every arXiv item opened at its abs page; Kaggle discussion pages are client-rendered and unreadable by fetch — their content comes from the ARC Prize blog, the Tufa blog, or search snippets, marked)

## 1. ARC-AGI-3 itself
- ARC Prize Foundation. "ARC-AGI-3: A New Challenge for Frontier Agentic Intelligence." arXiv:2603.24621 (Mar 2026). https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24621 — the benchmark, RHAE scoring, frontier systems <1% at launch; the protocol our capability-floor claim is stated against.
- Rodionov, S. "Executable World Models for ARC-AGI-3 in the Era of Coding Agents." arXiv:2605.05138. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05138 — GPT-5.5 builds/verifies Python world models, 15/25 public games fully solved; the handed-structure lineage we audited (works on a frontier model).
- Rodionov, S. "Do Coding Agents Need Executable World Models, Simplification, and Verification to Solve ARC-AGI-3?" arXiv:2607.15439. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15439 — score tracks model capability and reasoning effort; the added mechanisms help less than expected and non-uniformly; supports our null-to-negative read on handed structure.
- Liew, K. H. "Explore Before You Solve: The Speed–Depth Trade-off in Epistemic Agents for ARC-AGI-3." arXiv:2605.25931. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25931 — the 25 public games are solvable by non-intelligent strategies; a caveat on every public-set claim, ours included.
- Gao, Y., Guo, Y., Zong, Q., et al. "Safe, Persistent, and Evolving Agent Harness for Understanding Partially Observable Worlds" (E-Ledger / WorldAbduct). arXiv:2610.11552. https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11552 — abduces and verifies hidden rules from traces; a verified-facts ledger is the channel our model adopts. Not evaluated on ARC-AGI-3.
- Lehmann, J., Aioanei, A., Vahdati, S. "Tycho: Active Abstraction with Programmatic World Models for ARC-AGI-3." arXiv:2607.28287. https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.28287 — GPT-5.6 Sol and Opus 5 reach 100 RHAE on all 183 public levels; frontier-side evidence for the floor claim.
- Skoutnev, A., Acharya, K., Longhitano, G., Udell, M., Ellis, K., Drori, I. "Twin: Playing an Unknown Game with a Test-Time Digital Twin." arXiv:2608.14490. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.14490 — a counterexample-repaired executable twin lifts a base model from 7.8% to 93.3% (179/183 levels); the strongest "structure helps" result, on a frontier model. [base model to verify]
- Sarafian, E., Kaplun, G., Banner, R., Soudry, D., Ginsburg, B. "Workspace Optimization: How to Train Your Agent" (DreamTeam). arXiv:2605.09650. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09650 — evolves the external workspace, not weights: 36% → 38.4% protocol-matched.
- Karten, S., Zhang, A. L., et al. "Prime Agent: A Self-Improving RLM Harness." arXiv:2608.23552. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.23552 — persistent REPL + memories/skills raises ARC-AGI-3 RHAE Best@1 from 30% to 95.5% with no weight updates. [base model to verify — decisive for our claim]
- Tufa Labs. "The Duck" harness: Kaggle write-up https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/arc-prize-2026-arc-agi-3/discussion/717133 (unreadable by fetch); blog https://www.tufalabs.ai/research/duck-harness/ (Qwen 3.6 27B FP8, 1.60 ± 0.45 over 25 games × 20 passes, oldest-message eviction); code https://github.com/Tufalabs/duck-harness. ARC Prize Milestone 1 post https://arcprize.org/blog/arc-prize-2026-milestone-1: hand-crafted tools "actually hurt the model"; gains from multimodality and base models — precedent for our tools-read-negative result.
- Franzen, D. "ARC-AGI-3 Milestone 2: Building on the Duck Harness." https://github.com/tonghuikang/daniel-franzen-arc-agi-3/blob/main/WRITEUP.md — public LB 27.89, Qwen3.8-Flash-Next on one RTX PRO 6000, no training; the frozen-model baseline our paper sits on. Milestone 2 winners thread https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/arc-prize-2026-arc-agi-3/discussion/744792 (dfranzen 27.89, lordhansolo 23.84, sirikilohit 22.53; snippet only).

- OpenAI. "How enabling two settings tripled our scores on the ARC-AGI-3 benchmark." Jul 29 2026. https://openai.com/index/how-two-settings-tripled-our-arc-agi-3-scores/ (403 to fetch; details via https://www.remio.ai/post/openai-says-two-api-settings-tripled-gpt-5-6-sols-arc-agi-3-score) — retained reasoning + compaction: 13.3% → 38.3% public, ~6× fewer output tokens; supports "the model's own reasoning is the channel".

## 8. Memento 3 (added 2026-10-27 from the PDF, the closest method to ours)
- Zhao, H., Yu, Z., He, Z., Fang, M., Tutunov, R., Bou-Ammar, H., Luo, W., Wang, J. "Memento 3: Model-Based Recursive Self-Improvement through Reflective Rulebooks." arXiv:2610.11794, 8 Oct 2026 (UCL, Huawei Noah's Ark UK, Liverpool). https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11794 — "Code as Model": a frozen LLM keeps a natural-language rulebook (world_model.md) as semantic memory and compiles it to an executable (world_model_engine.py); a loop of observation, reflection, rule revision, compilation and verification accepts a pair only when the LLM judges the code faithful to the rulebook AND cell-exact replay reproduces every recorded frame; a population extension keeps N rulebook–executable pairs in parallel. Harness built from the baseline1 codebase, run in Claude Code with Claude Opus 5 at extra-high effort. Result: every level of all 25 public games, mean RHAE 100.0, 7,518 actions = 0.44 of the human baseline; 59.3 points above Opus 5 in the ARC Prize Standard harness; Atari Pong 21:0 ×3 with a learned controller and no further LLM calls. Ablation removes the rulebook on one game per difficulty band. Git memory of every rulebook version.
- CAUTION for the memo and the paper: the sentence "Learning to act in unfamiliar environments requires agents to infer how the world works and revise that understanding as new evidence arrives" is the verbatim first sentence of Memento 3's abstract. It must be paraphrased wherever we use it.

## 9. Added 2026-10-28 (verified at the abs pages; relayed by the lead's reviewer)
- Wu, C., Passenger, J., Song, Y. "Tracing the Thoughts of a Coding Agent Playing ARC-AGI-3: Lessons for Continual Learning." arXiv:2610.11450 (8 Oct 2026; NeurIPS 2026 CL4FMAgents workshop). https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11450 — a frozen model whose only memory is the files it writes; only 33 of 630 script references cross a task boundary and 74% of pre-boundary scripts are abandoned; notes are append-only and contradictions are settled against the log; seven runs, three backbones, two families. The independent observation that written structure does not travel across tasks; our +5.5 same-run cards are the positive residual they do not measure.
- Lencevicius, R. "When the harness decides success: state that cannot be re-observed." arXiv:2610.26632 (19 Oct 2026, Microsoft). https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26632 — truncating context costs little when a fact can be re-observed and drops success to chance when it cannot (92.5-point gap, 90% CI [89.5, 95.5]); on ARC-AGI-3, one model, four games: summarising old turns while keeping the note-taking invitation recovers 86% of a 63-point Standard-vs-Adapter gap without replaying reasoning; removing the invitation recovers 43%; native reasoning replay the remaining 57% (single-run point estimates). Our own-notes channel is the measured piece of that mechanism under the open-model constraint.
